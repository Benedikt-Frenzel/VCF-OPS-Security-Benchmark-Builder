# AI Transparency Notice

This repository was developed with substantial assistance from generative
artificial intelligence. Documentation, source code, and the curated
decision-matrix content (rule descriptions and recommendations) were produced
or co-produced with large language models (LLMs).

Under Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the EU AI Act), applicable since
2 August 2026, AI-generated text that is published for the purpose of informing
the public must be disclosed as such. Accordingly, AI-generated or AI-assisted
text in this repository is disclosed here as AI-generated.

The tool itself is deterministic at runtime: it transforms a curated decision
matrix into VMware Aria Operations symptom and alert definitions and does not
invoke any AI system.
